Lucky Wheels Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy describes how the mobile game Lucky Wheels (package com.medvejut.luckywheels), published by medvejut, handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit or share any personal or usage information. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no purchases.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress, such as your level and coins, and your control settings in its own storage on your device. We do not receive this information. It is deleted when you clear the game's data or uninstall the game. If device backup is enabled in your system settings, your operating system may include this data in your own backup.

Permissions

The game declares the internet permission, which the game engine adds. The current version does not use it to send information about you. The game does not ask for access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Google Play

When you install the game or join a test through Google Play, Google handles information under its own Privacy Policy. Google may provide us with statistics about installs and crashes.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no information about you, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete. To remove the saved progress, clear the game's data or uninstall the game.

Changes

If a later version starts to collect information, for example through advertising or analytics, we will update this page before releasing that version.
